# A note with no links is nearly worthless

A note nobody can reach is functionally deleted. Folders tell you *where* something lives; links
are what make it findable six months later, when you've forgotten the note exists and only
remember the thing next to it.

This is also what makes the vault useful to a model. Grep finds notes that share your exact
words. Links find notes that share an *idea* — which is usually what you actually wanted.

So: before writing a new note, search for what's already here and connect to it. One good link
beats a perfect folder.
