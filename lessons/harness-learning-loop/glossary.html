Glossary

Fixed meanings for this course. Where a term already has a meaning in AGENTS.md, that one wins and the citation is given.

TermMeaning
ChannelA route by which text reaches the model before it answers. Three exist in Claude Code: SessionStart, UserPromptSubmit, UserPromptExpansion.
InjectionText a hook prints that the harness places in context. Always labelled, so it never reads as something the human said.
MatcherA filter on a hook event. For SessionStart: startup, resume, clear, compact, fork.
DecayAn instruction given once losing to everything said since. The failure the per-turn channel exists to prevent. AGENTS.md:82.
Contract vs contextContract is re-stated every turn because it must not drift. Context is read once. Three sections of the agreement are contract; the rest is context. AGENTS.md:80.
Per-session budget40 lines, the [[About me]] cap. Stable, expensive, read once.
Per-turn budget15 lines, the agreement cap. Perishable, cheap per line, re-sent on every prompt. AGENTS.md:111.
ProposalAn offer to write down something the human said. Never written unilaterally. AGENTS.md:132.
AssumptionA labelled conclusion the vault reached about the human, with a falsifier, in the register. Never a proposal. AGENTS.md:163.
FrictionAn observable moment of the brain getting it wrong: a magic word, a correction, a re-ask. Currently recorded nowhere.
DeclineA proposal the human did not take up. Today: dropped. Proposed: evidence that the wording was wrong, not that the preference is absent.
The one-way ruleAn assumption never becomes a fact by age, repetition or usefulness. Only the human promotes it. AGENTS.md:578.
